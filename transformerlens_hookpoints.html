TransformerLens — watch the cache fill up

An activation is any intermediate number the model computes on the way to its answer. Normally they are thrown away. run_with_cache keeps every one of them, labelled by name. ① Intuition walks the forward pass stage by stage; ② Code shows the few lines that do all of it at once. Click any cache entry to open it. Axis labels like B5 mean token B at position 5.
cache["pattern", 0] is shorthand for cache["blocks.0.attn.hook_pattern"] — utils.get_act_name("pattern", 0) builds that string. Once you can read these names, run_with_hooks(tokens, fwd_hooks=[(name, fn)]) is just “pick a name and intercept it” (hooks page).
