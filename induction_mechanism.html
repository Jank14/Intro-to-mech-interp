The induction algorithm — one position at a time

Two heads, two layers: 0.7 tags every token with “who came before me”, 1.4 searches for a matching tag, then copies what it found. Keys: → next step · ← back Axis labels like B5 mean token B at position 5.
Why care? “Last time I saw this token, what came next? Do that again” is in-context learning in its simplest form — the model completes a pattern it has never seen, with no training. It is the first full circuit taught in mech interp because we can name both heads and prove it.

The third common head type — first-token / “resting” heads — attends to position 0 (BOS) when there is nothing useful to do. In the real toy model, 1.4 and 1.10 rest on BOS before the repeat; this idealized version just spreads its attention instead.
