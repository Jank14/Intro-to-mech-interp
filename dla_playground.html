Direct Logit Attribution — who pushed the answer?

The residual stream is a sum of every component’s output, and the logit for a word is that sum · W_U[:, word] (the unembedding column for that word). So each component’s share of a logit is one dot product.
Toy model: vocabulary of 6 tokens A–F, sequence A B C D A B C D (the repeat starts at position 4). A small number after a token is its position: C6 = token C at position 6.
In the real notebook the direct path (embed → unembed) carries surprisingly large logits for frequent, predictable tokens — so don’t skip the grey bar. And DLA only measures the direct push: a head that helps by feeding a later head (like 0.7 → 1.4) scores ≈ 0 here. That is why we also need ablations.
